# Sample Contract for Testing

## SERVICE AGREEMENT

**This Service Agreement** ("Agreement") is entered into on January 15, 2026 ("Effective Date")

**BETWEEN:**

TechStart Solutions Private Limited, a company incorporated under the Companies Act, 2013, having its registered office at 123 MG Road, Bangalore, Karnataka - 560001 ("Service Provider")

**AND**

ABC Manufacturing Pvt Ltd, a company incorporated under the Companies Act, 2013, having its registered office at 456 Industrial Area, Mumbai, Maharashtra - 400001 ("Client")

---

## 1. SCOPE OF SERVICES

1.1 The Service Provider shall provide software development and maintenance services as detailed in Schedule A attached hereto.

1.2 The Service Provider shall deliver the completed software modules within 6 months from the Effective Date.

---

## 2. PAYMENT TERMS

2.1 The Client shall pay the Service Provider a total fee of Rs. 25,00,000 (Rupees Twenty-Five Lakhs only) as follows:
   - 30% upon signing (Rs. 7,50,000)
   - 40% upon delivery of Phase 1 (Rs. 10,00,000)
   - 30% upon final delivery and acceptance (Rs. 7,50,000)

2.2 All payments shall be made within 15 days of invoice receipt.

2.3 Late payments shall attract a penalty of 2% per month.

---

## 3. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY RIGHTS

3.1 All intellectual property rights in the software developed under this Agreement shall be exclusively owned by the Client upon full payment.

3.2 The Service Provider hereby assigns, transfers, and conveys to the Client all rights, title, and interest in and to all work product.

3.3 The Service Provider waives all moral rights in the work product.

---

## 4. CONFIDENTIALITY

4.1 The Service Provider acknowledges that it may receive confidential information from the Client during the course of this engagement.

4.2 The Service Provider shall maintain strict confidentiality and shall not disclose any confidential information to third parties.

4.3 This confidentiality obligation shall survive in perpetuity.

4.4 Breach of confidentiality may result in immediate termination and liquidated damages of Rs. 50,00,000.

---

## 5. LIABILITY AND INDEMNIFICATION

5.1 The Service Provider shall indemnify and hold harmless the Client from any and all claims, damages, losses, and expenses arising from the Service Provider's performance of services under this Agreement.

5.2 The Service Provider's liability under this Agreement shall be unlimited and shall include all direct, indirect, consequential, and punitive damages.

5.3 The Service Provider shall obtain and maintain professional liability insurance with coverage of not less than Rs. 1,00,00,000.

---

## 6. NON-COMPETE AND NON-SOLICITATION

6.1 During the term of this Agreement and for a period of 5 years thereafter, the Service Provider shall not:
   a) Engage in any business that competes with the Client
   b) Solicit or attempt to solicit any customers or clients of the Client
   c) Employ or engage any person who is or was employed by the Client

6.2 This restriction applies globally.

---

## 7. TERMINATION

7.1 The Client may terminate this Agreement at any time, with or without cause, by providing written notice to the Service Provider.

7.2 Upon termination by the Client, the Service Provider shall immediately cease all work and deliver all completed and incomplete work product to the Client.

7.3 The Client shall have no obligation to pay for work in progress at the time of termination.

7.4 The Service Provider may only terminate this Agreement for material breach by the Client, and only if such breach remains uncured for 90 days after written notice.

---

## 8. TERM AND RENEWAL

8.1 This Agreement shall commence on the Effective Date and continue for an initial term of 2 years.

8.2 This Agreement shall automatically renew for successive 2-year terms unless the Service Provider provides written notice of non-renewal at least 180 days prior to the end of the then-current term.

8.3 Failure to provide timely notice shall result in automatic renewal, and early termination during a renewal term shall result in a penalty equal to 50% of the remaining contract value.

---

## 9. WARRANTIES

9.1 The Service Provider warrants that:
   a) The software will be free from defects for a period of 3 years
   b) The software will meet all specifications and requirements as determined by the Client in its sole discretion
   c) The software will operate without interruption or error

9.2 The Service Provider provides unlimited warranty support at no additional cost.

9.3 Any defects must be corrected within 24 hours of notification.

---

## 10. DISPUTE RESOLUTION

10.1 Any disputes arising under this Agreement shall be subject to arbitration in London, United Kingdom.

10.2 The arbitration shall be conducted in accordance with the rules of the London Court of International Arbitration.

10.3 Each party shall bear its own costs, and the losing party shall pay all of the prevailing party's legal fees and costs.

10.4 This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of England and Wales.

---

## 11. CHANGES AND AMENDMENTS

11.1 The Client may request changes to the scope of services at any time.

11.2 The Service Provider shall accommodate all change requests without additional charge, provided the total changes do not exceed reasonable limits as determined by the Client.

11.3 Changes requested by the Client shall not extend the delivery timeline.

---

## 12. REPRESENTATIONS

12.1 The Service Provider represents and warrants that it has all necessary expertise, resources, and capabilities to perform the services.

12.2 The Service Provider represents that the software will be suitable for all purposes, known or unknown.

---

## 13. GENERAL PROVISIONS

13.1 This Agreement may be amended by the Client at any time by providing written notice to the Service Provider.

13.2 The Service Provider's continued performance after such amendment shall constitute acceptance.

13.3 This Agreement shall be binding upon and inure to the benefit of the parties and their respective successors and assigns; provided that the Service Provider may not assign this Agreement without the prior written consent of the Client.

---
