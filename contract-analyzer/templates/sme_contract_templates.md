# SME-Friendly Contract Clause Templates

## Balanced Liability Clause

**Template:**
```
Each party's total liability under this Agreement, whether in contract, tort, or otherwise, 
shall not exceed [X amount or Y% of the total contract value], except in cases of:
a) Willful misconduct or gross negligence
b) Breach of confidentiality obligations
c) Infringement of intellectual property rights
```

**Why This Protects SMEs:**
- Sets a clear financial cap on liability
- Prevents unlimited exposure
- Still holds parties accountable for serious breaches

---

## Mutual Termination Rights

**Template:**
```
Either party may terminate this Agreement:
a) For convenience, by providing [30/60/90] days written notice to the other party
b) For cause, immediately upon written notice if the other party materially breaches 
   this Agreement and fails to cure such breach within [15/30] days of receiving 
   written notice specifying the breach
```

**Why This Protects SMEs:**
- Provides exit options if business needs change
- Ensures mutual rights (not one-sided)
- Allows time to cure breaches before termination

---

## Reasonable Payment Terms

**Template:**
```
Payment Terms:
a) Invoice submission: Within [5] days of delivery/completion
b) Payment due: [30/45/60] days from invoice date
c) Late payment interest: [X%] per month or the maximum allowed by law, whichever is lower
d) Dispute resolution: Any disputed amounts must be raised within [10] days; 
   undisputed amounts remain payable
```

**Why This Protects SMEs:**
- Clear timeline protects cash flow
- Late fees discourage payment delays
- Dispute process prevents withholding entire payment

---

## Limited Indemnification

**Template:**
```
Each party shall indemnify and hold harmless the other party from claims, damages, 
and expenses arising solely from:
a) The indemnifying party's negligence or willful misconduct
b) The indemnifying party's breach of its representations and warranties
c) The indemnifying party's violation of applicable laws

Indemnification obligations are subject to the liability limitations set forth in 
this Agreement and do not apply to claims arising from the indemnified party's own acts.
```

**Why This Protects SMEs:**
- Limits indemnity to party's own actions
- Prevents broad, one-sided indemnification
- Subject to overall liability caps

## Balanced Confidentiality

**Template:**
```
Both parties agree to:
a) Maintain confidentiality of information marked "Confidential" or disclosed under 
   circumstances indicating confidential nature
b) Use Confidential Information only for purposes of this Agreement
c) Return or destroy Confidential Information upon termination

Confidential Information does NOT include information that:
- Is publicly available through no fault of receiving party
- Was known to receiving party before disclosure
- Is independently developed without use of Confidential Information
- Is required to be disclosed by law (with notice to disclosing party)

Duration: [2/3/5] years from date of disclosure
```

**Why This Protects SMEs:**
- Reciprocal obligations (both parties bound equally)
- Clear exclusions prevent overreach
- Time limit on confidentiality obligations

## IP Rights - License Model

**Template:**
```
Intellectual Property Rights:
a) Pre-existing IP: Each party retains ownership of its pre-existing intellectual property
b) Work Product: [Service Provider] grants [Client] a perpetual, worldwide, 
   non-exclusive, royalty-free license to use all deliverables created under this Agreement
c) Modifications: [Client] may modify deliverables for internal business purposes
d) Restrictions: [Client] shall not resell, redistribute, or use deliverables to 
   compete with [Service Provider]
```

**Why This Protects SMEs:**
- Client gets usage rights without excessive cost
- Provider retains IP for reuse with other clients
- Balanced approach vs. full IP transfer

## Auto-Renewal with Exit Option

**Template:**
```
Term and Renewal:
a) Initial Term: [1/2/3] year(s) from Effective Date
b) Renewal: Automatically renews for successive [1-year] terms unless either party 
   provides written notice of non-renewal at least [30/60/90] days before the end 
   of the then-current term
c) No penalty for non-renewal
```

**Why This Protects SMEs:**
- Easy exit without penalty
- Reasonable notice period to find alternatives
- Prevents lock-in situations

## Limited Non-Compete

**Template:**
```
Non-Compete (applicable only to [Party Name]):
During the term and for [6/12] months thereafter, [Party] agrees not to directly 
provide [specific services/products] that directly compete with [Other Party] within 
[specific geographic area/market segment].

This restriction does NOT prevent [Party] from:
- Engaging in other business activities
- Serving existing clients
- Providing non-competing services
```

**Why This Protects SMEs:**
- Limited in time, geography, and scope
- Doesn't prevent all business activity
- Protects legitimate competitive interests only

## Clear Performance Standards

**Template:**
```
Performance Standards:
a) [Service/Product] shall meet the specifications set forth in Exhibit A
b) Delivery/completion by [specific date or within X days of order]
c) Acceptance: [Client] has [5/10] business days to test and accept or reject 
   with specific reasons
d) Remedies: If rejected, [Provider] has [X] days to cure deficiencies at no 
   additional cost

Service Level Agreement (if applicable):
- Uptime: [99.X%]
- Response time: [X hours] for critical issues
- Credits: [X%] of monthly fees for each [Y%] below SLA
```

**Why This Protects SMEs:**
- Clear, measurable standards
- Defined acceptance process
- Remedies for non-performance

## Dispute Resolution (India-Specific)

**Template:**
```
Dispute Resolution:
a) Negotiation: Parties shall first attempt to resolve disputes through good faith 
   negotiation between senior executives
b) Mediation: If unresolved within [30] days, parties shall attempt mediation with 
   a mutually agreed mediator
c) Arbitration: If mediation fails, disputes shall be resolved by arbitration under 
   the Arbitration and Conciliation Act, 1996
   - Seat: [City], India
   - Language: English
   - Number of arbitrators: [1 or 3]
   - Costs: Each party bears its own costs; arbitrator fees split equally

Governing Law: Laws of India
Jurisdiction: Courts of [City], India (for enforcement of arbitration awards only)
```

**Why This Protects SMEs:**
- Escalation process from negotiation to arbitration
- India-specific provisions
- Cost-sharing prevents one party from forcing expensive litigation

## Force Majeure

**Template:**
```
Force Majeure:
Neither party shall be liable for failure to perform obligations due to events beyond 
reasonable control, including:
- Natural disasters, epidemics, pandemics
- War, terrorism, civil unrest
- Government actions, strikes, labor disputes
- Failure of utilities or internet services

Obligations:
a) Affected party must notify other party within [5] days
b) Parties shall work together to minimize impact
c) If force majeure continues beyond [60/90] days, either party may terminate 
   without penalty

This clause does not excuse payment obligations for services already rendered.
```

**Why This Protects SMEs:**
- Protects from liability for uncontrollable events
- Mutual protection (not one-sided)
- Includes modern risks (pandemics, internet failures)
- Limits indefinite suspension

## Usage Guidelines

**When to Use These Templates:**
1. During contract negotiations as alternative language
2. When you identify unfavorable clauses in contracts you receive
3. As a starting point for drafting your own contracts
4. In discussions with legal counsel

**Important Notes:**
- These are templates, not legal advice
- Customize based on your specific situation
- Always have contracts reviewed by a lawyer before signing
- Consider your industry's specific requirements and norms
